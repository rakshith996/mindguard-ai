Your choices and privacy

MindGuard AI is a college prototype intended to support reflection and help students connect with people who can support them. It does not diagnose, predict, or treat mental-health conditions.

  • Daily check-ins ask about mood, stress, sleep and academic pressure.
  • Your check-in history is visible to you. It is not shown to general administrators.
  • Counsellors see appointment requests you choose to send and de-identified cohort summaries only. Small groups are hidden.
  • Participation is voluntary. You may skip a check-in, withdraw consent and erase live check-in/request records, or request account deletion from Privacy Settings.
  • Do not use this website for emergencies. It is not monitored continuously.

Before agreeing, understand that this is a classroom prototype, not a continuously monitored service. The project team must explain its data-retention and backup-deletion schedule before any real student deployment.

If you are in immediate danger or may harm yourself, contact emergency services or a qualified person now. In India, call 112; Tele-MANAS: 14416.
