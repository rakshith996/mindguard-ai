STUDENT-CONTROLLED SETTINGS

Your data and your choices

You can stop participating at any time. These actions require your current password so another person using an open session cannot make the change casually.

What the prototype stores: your account details, consent timestamp/version, check-in ratings (mood, stress, sleep and academic pressure), and appointment requests you intentionally submit. It does not store the immediate-danger answer.

Withdraw consent and erase wellness records

This removes your check-ins and appointment requests from the live MindGuard database and clears your consent record. Your login account remains, and you will be asked for consent again before using student features. Any counsellor may already have seen an appointment request you sent; this action cannot erase anything copied outside this prototype database or retained in backups.

Delete my account permanently

This permanently deletes your student account and linked check-ins and appointment requests from the live database. You will not be able to sign in again with this account. Create a new account only if you later choose to use the prototype again. Database backups and any information already viewed or copied by a recipient are outside this demo's automatic deletion process.
